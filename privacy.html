Mockup Camera — Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Mockup Camera ("the app") is built so your content stays on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests.

Camera

The camera is used only to show your design on real objects in real time. Camera frames are processed on your iPhone and are never recorded or transmitted unless you take a photo or video yourself.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record a video, to include sound in that video. Audio is saved only into the video you create.

Photos

If you import a design, the app reads only the image you pick. If you save a capture, the app adds it to your photo library ("Add Photos Only" permission) and cannot read your other photos.

Your designs

Designs you import are stored inside the app on your device so you can reuse them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
