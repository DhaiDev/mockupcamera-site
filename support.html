Tips for the best results

  • Use good, even lighting and move your iPhone slowly for a few seconds when the camera opens.
  • For mugs, bottles and cans, stand the object on a table and look slightly down at it.
  • Tap anything to pin the design there; tap the chip at the top to go back to automatic mode.
  • Use Adjust → Realism to tune light, texture and shine.
  • iPhone Pro models with LiDAR detect curved objects most accurately.

Common questions

The design doesn't appear. Make sure camera access is on in Settings → Mockup Camera, and point at a textured surface in good light.

Captures aren't saved. Allow "Add Photos Only" in Settings → Mockup Camera → Photos.

Videos have no sound. Allow microphone access in Settings → Mockup Camera.
